Sezam BBS – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Summary

Sezam BBS does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

Everything stays on your device

The message archive and the only app preference (font size) are stored only on your device. Nothing is sent to the developer.

No network access

The archive ships inside the app. The app makes no network connections.

Archive content is public-domain

The app operates on a read-only copy of public messages and member listings from the Sezam BBS (1989–1999), taken from the public-domain oldsezam.net site.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.
